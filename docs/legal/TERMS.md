## 1. Who agrees to this

The agreement is between [LEGAL ENTITY] and the **school or organisation**, not

## 2. What the service is

A classroom token-and-cards platform: teachers award tokens, students spend them
drawing from a shared deck, and cards are redeemed for whatever the teacher has
decided they are worth.

**The rewards are the school's.** If a card says "skip one homework", honouring
that is entirely between the teacher and the student. We record that a card was
used; we have no part in what it entitles anyone to.

## 3. Accounts

The school is responsible for who has an account. Teacher accounts exist by
invitation from a school administrator — there is no public sign-up. Student
accounts are created by teachers.

Credentials are shown once at creation. We cannot recover a password; a teacher
resets it. The school is responsible for distributing credentials sensibly and
for removing accounts of people who have left.

## 4. Acceptable use

Do not use the service to harass, bully or single out a student; to store
personal data beyond what it asks for (there are no free-text fields for a
reason); to upload card artwork you do not have the rights to, or that is not
appropriate for children; or to attack, probe or overload the service.

Card images are uploaded by teachers. **The school is responsible for what its
teachers upload**, including copyright and suitability. We may remove content
that is unlawful or plainly inappropriate for a school.

## 5. Data protection

separately and takes precedence over this section where they differ.

We do not use student data to train models, to advertise, or for any purpose
other than providing the service.
